Rules (summary)

  • Free to enter. Open to individuals and teams of up to 4. Participants under 18 need a parent's or guardian's consent.
  • Your work must be original; you keep all IP in your app.
  • No real-money gaming, betting, lending or apps that break Indian law or store policies.
  • Submit a demo video (≤3 min), a link to the app/prototype and a short write-up.
  • Winners are chosen by an editorial panel; decisions are final. Prizes are subject to sponsor confirmation and applicable taxes.
  • We may feature submissions (with credit) on AppBharat.
