Government apps

16 Government Apps Every Indian Should Have on Their Phone

The official apps that save time and trips to offices: documents, identity, tax, travel, health, safety and elections.

By AppBharat Editorial · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 7 min read ·

Government services in India have moved to mobile in a big way. These official apps can save you hours in queues — as long as you install the genuine versions from official stores or department websites.

Identity and documents

  • DigiLocker — official digital copies of your licence, RC, mark sheets and more. Read our guide.
  • mAadhaar — carry your Aadhaar profile, lock biometrics and download e-Aadhaar.
  • mPassport Seva — apply for a passport and track your application.

Services in one place

  • UMANG — a single app for many central and state services, including EPFO passbook and claims.

Money and tax

  • BHIM — NPCI's own UPI app.
  • AIS for Taxpayer — view your Annual Information Statement before filing returns.

Travel and transport

  • IRCTC Rail Connect — book reserved train tickets.
  • UTS — unreserved, platform and season tickets.
  • RailMadad — get help and register complaints during rail journeys.
  • mParivahan — virtual licence and RC, vehicle lookups.

Health

  • ABHA — your Ayushman Bharat health account for linking records.
  • Jan Aushadhi Sugam — find affordable generic medicine stores.

Safety and weather

  • Sanchar Saathi — report fraud calls and messages, block lost phones and see connections issued in your name.
  • Mausam and Damini — official weather forecasts and lightning alerts.

Citizenship

  • Voter Helpline — check your name on the electoral roll and apply for corrections.
Stay safe: Government apps never ask for payment to "activate" your account, and officials will not ask for your OTP over the phone.
