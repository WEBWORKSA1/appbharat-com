How to Launch an App in India: Step-by-Step from Idea to Play Store

A practical roadmap for first-time founders: validation, company setup, building an MVP, Play Store and App Store publishing, and getting your first 1,000 users.

By AppBharat Editorial · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 8 min read ·

Launching an app in India has never been more accessible — affordable development, UPI for payments and a huge mobile-first audience. But most apps fail for avoidable reasons: they solve the wrong problem, launch too late or never reach users. Here is a roadmap that avoids those traps.

1. Validate before you build

Talk to at least ten people who have the problem. Ask how they solve it today and what it costs them. If nobody is hacking together a workaround, the problem may not be painful enough. A simple landing page with a waitlist is a cheap test of demand.

2. Set up the business basics

  • Choose a structure — sole proprietorship to start quickly, or LLP/Private Limited if you plan to raise investment.
  • Open a current account and check GST registration requirements.
  • Search the trademark registry for your app name and file early if it's available.
  • Prepare a privacy policy and terms of use. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act requires clear, consent-based handling of personal data.

3. Define the MVP

Write down the single most important job your app does. Cut everything else. A focused MVP is cheaper, faster and teaches you more. Our cost calculator shows how features change your budget.

4. Build smart

Choose a cross-platform framework unless you need deep native features. Optimise for mid-range Android phones and patchy networks — that's the reality for most Indian users. Ask for only the permissions you need; users increasingly uninstall apps that over-ask.

5. Publish on the stores

  • Google Play: create a developer account (US$25 one-time), complete the Data Safety form and content rating, and run a closed test before production. New personal developer accounts must complete a testing period with real testers before publishing.
  • Apple App Store: join the Apple Developer Program (US$99/year), complete privacy "nutrition labels" and follow the Human Interface Guidelines to avoid rejections.

6. Get your first 1,000 users

  1. Onboard the people you interviewed — they're your first fans.
  2. Create short demo videos in Hindi and English for Instagram, YouTube Shorts and WhatsApp.
  3. Post in relevant communities with a genuine story, not just a link.
  4. Submit your app to directories, including AppBharat.
  5. Ask satisfied users for store reviews from inside the app.

7. Measure and iterate

Track activation and retention, not just downloads. Fix the biggest drop-off each week. Grab our free India App Launch Checklist to keep everything on track.
