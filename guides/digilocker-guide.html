DigiLocker Guide: How to Store and Share Your Documents Digitally

What DigiLocker is, which documents you can get in it, how to sign up, and where digital documents are accepted instead of originals.

By AppBharat Editorial · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 6 min read ·

DigiLocker is the Government of India's digital document wallet, run by the Ministry of Electronics and IT. Instead of carrying paper copies of your driving licence, vehicle RC or mark sheets, you can fetch official digital versions directly from the issuing departments and show or share them when needed.

Issued documents vs uploaded documents

This is the most important thing to understand. Issued documents are pulled straight from the issuing authority's database — for example, your driving licence from the transport department or your CBSE mark sheet from the board. DigiLocker states that these issued documents are treated as equivalent to original physical documents under the IT rules. Uploaded documents are scans you add yourself; they are handy for your own records but do not carry the same legal standing.

Popular documents you can fetch

  • Aadhaar (from UIDAI)
  • PAN verification record
  • Driving licence and vehicle registration certificate
  • Class 10 and 12 mark sheets from many boards, and degree certificates from participating universities
  • Insurance policies from participating insurers
  • Various state certificates (income, caste, domicile) where states have integrated

Availability depends on whether the issuing department has joined DigiLocker, so the list grows over time.

How to sign up

  1. Install DigiLocker from the official store listing or visit digilocker.gov.in.
  2. Sign up with your mobile number and verify the OTP.
  3. Link your Aadhaar to verify your identity.
  4. Go to "Search documents", choose the issuer (e.g. your state transport department) and enter the requested details to fetch the document.

Where it's useful

Traffic police can verify your licence and RC digitally, many universities and employers accept DigiLocker documents, and you can share a document with a time-limited link instead of emailing scans. It is also a safe backup if you lose your wallet.

Safety tips

  • Only use the official app or website — never a "DigiLocker" link sent on WhatsApp.
  • Set a strong security PIN and never share your OTP.
  • Review "Issued documents" and remove access you no longer need.
