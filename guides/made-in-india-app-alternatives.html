Made-in-India Alternatives to Popular Apps: What's Worth Switching To

Indian alternatives to WhatsApp, Gmail, Google Maps, Zoom, Spotify, Uber and Microsoft Office — what each does well and when switching makes sense.

By AppBharat Editorial · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 6 min read ·

Interest in Indian-built apps has grown steadily, driven by pride in local products, data-localisation concerns and simply better fits for Indian needs like regional languages and UPI. But switching apps has costs: your contacts, files and habits live in the old app. Here's a practical look at what's worth switching to.

Messaging: Arattai (Zoho)

Arattai offers chats, voice and video calls, stories and channels from Chennai-based Zoho. The challenge with any messenger is network effects — it is only useful when your contacts join. Start with a family or team group.

Email and office: Zoho Mail and Zoho Workplace

For businesses, Zoho's suite is a mature alternative to Gmail/Google Workspace and Microsoft 365, with custom-domain email, documents, spreadsheets and team chat (Zoho Cliq). It is ad-free and competitively priced. This is one of the easiest switches for small companies.

Maps: Mappls by MapmyIndia

MapmyIndia has mapped India for decades, and Mappls includes India-specific features such as junction views and road-safety alerts. Try it side by side with your current maps app on your regular routes.

Video meetings: JioMeet

JioMeet covers everyday meetings and classes. For large enterprise webinars, compare features carefully before switching.

Music and audio: JioSaavn, Gaana, Pocket FM, Kuku FM

Indian music apps have deep Bollywood and regional catalogues. Pocket FM and Kuku FM have built a large audience for audio series and audiobooks in Indian languages.

Rides: Ola, Rapido, Namma Yatri

Rapido's bike taxis are often the cheapest option in traffic-heavy cities, and Namma Yatri's zero-commission model is popular with auto drivers in several cities.

Translation: Bhashini

The government-backed Bhashini mission offers translation and voice tools across many Indian languages, useful where global tools are weaker.

How to switch without pain

  1. Switch one category at a time.
  2. Run both apps in parallel for two weeks.
  3. Export data (contacts, files, playlists) before deleting anything.
  4. Bring your group along — share the app with family or colleagues.
