How Much Does It Cost to Build an App in India? (2026 Guide)

Realistic app development cost ranges in India by complexity, platform and team — plus hidden costs and how to cut your budget without cutting quality.

By AppBharat Editorial · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 8 min read ·

In this guide: cost ranges · what drives cost · freelancer vs agency · hidden costs · how to save · next steps

"How much will my app cost?" is the first question every founder asks, and the honest answer is: it depends on what you build, where it runs and who builds it. India remains one of the most cost-effective places in the world to build software, but prices still vary tenfold between a template app from a freelancer and a custom product from a top studio. This guide gives you working numbers so you can plan.

Typical cost ranges in India

These are indicative ranges for design, development, testing and launch by a mid-size Indian agency. Freelancers can be 30–50% cheaper; premium studios can be 1.5–2× more. For a personalised range, use our app cost calculator.

What actually drives the cost

  • Number of user roles. A food delivery app is really three apps — customer, delivery partner and restaurant — plus an admin panel. Each role multiplies screens and logic.
  • Platforms. One cross-platform codebase (Flutter or React Native) for Android and iOS usually costs about 30% more than a single platform, while two separate native apps can cost 70–80% more.
  • Integrations. UPI and card payments, maps, SMS/OTP, WhatsApp messaging, KYC providers and ERPs each add design, development and testing time.
  • Real-time features. Chat, live location and live auctions need specialised backend infrastructure.
  • AI features. Chatbots, recommendations or document reading add model costs, data work and careful testing.
  • Design depth. A template UI is cheap; a distinctive brand experience with motion and illustrations takes skilled designers.
  • Compliance. Fintech, health and children's apps carry extra security and regulatory work.

Freelancer, agency or in-house?

Freelancers suit small, well-defined apps and tight budgets, but you take on project management and the risk that one person becomes unavailable. Agencies bring a full team — designer, developers, QA and project manager — and are the most common choice for MVPs. In-house teams make sense once you have product-market fit and need to iterate every week; a small in-house team in a metro can cost ₹40–80 lakh a year including salaries and tools.

Hidden costs most founders miss

  • Store fees: Google Play charges a one-time US$25 registration fee; the Apple Developer Program costs US$99 per year.
  • Hosting and services: servers, databases, storage, SMS/OTP, email and maps APIs — anywhere from a few thousand to lakhs of rupees per month as you scale.
  • Maintenance: budget roughly 15–20% of the build cost every year for bug fixes, OS updates and security patches.
  • Marketing: building the app is often less than half the cost of getting users.
  • Legal: privacy policy, terms, company registration and trademark filing.

How to reduce cost without hurting quality

  1. Ship an MVP. Build the one workflow users pay for. Everything else is version 2.
  2. Go cross-platform. For most business apps, Flutter or React Native is good enough and saves money.
  3. Use proven services. Use payment gateways, authentication and analytics services instead of building your own.
  4. Write a clear brief. Vague scope is the biggest cause of overruns. User flows and a feature list cut estimates and arguments.
  5. Pay by milestone. Tie payments to working, demoed features.
