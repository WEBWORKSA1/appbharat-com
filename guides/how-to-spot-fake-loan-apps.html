How to Spot Fake Loan Apps in India — and What to Do If You're Targeted

Warning signs of illegal digital lending apps, how to check whether a lender is regulated, and steps to take if you face harassment.

By AppBharat Editorial · Updated 17 Nov 2026 · 6 min read ·

Instant-loan apps can be useful, but illegal lenders use them to trap borrowers with hidden charges, abusive recovery and misuse of personal data. Knowing the warning signs takes two minutes and can save you from serious harm.

Legitimate digital lending in brief

In India, lending must be done by entities regulated by the Reserve Bank of India — banks and registered NBFCs — or through apps that partner with them. Under RBI's digital lending rules, the lender's name should be disclosed upfront, you should get a Key Fact Statement showing the total cost of the loan including all fees, and loan money should move directly between your bank account and the regulated lender.

Red flags of a fake loan app

  • "No CIBIL, no documents, loan in 5 minutes" with no named lending partner.
  • Asks for access to your contacts, photos or call logs.
  • Deducts large "processing fees" upfront, so you receive far less than the loan amount.
  • Very short tenures (7–15 days) with unclear interest.
  • Download links shared through SMS or social media rather than official stores.
  • No physical address, grievance officer or privacy policy.
  • Recovery agents threaten you or message your contacts.

How to check a lender

  1. Find the name of the bank or NBFC behind the app — it should be stated clearly.
  2. Check that the entity is regulated on the RBI website.
  3. Read the Key Fact Statement before accepting; compare the annual percentage rate.
  4. Look for real reviews, and be wary of many new five-star reviews.

If you're being harassed

  • Do not pay further amounts under threat.
  • Save screenshots of messages, call logs and payment proofs.
  • Report to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in) or call 1930, and approach your local police.
  • Unauthorised entities can be reported on RBI's Sachet portal.
  • Revoke the app's permissions, uninstall it and change your passwords.
