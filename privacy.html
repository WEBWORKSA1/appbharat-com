Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how AppBharat.com ("AppBharat", "we") handles information when you use this website.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — when you submit a form (quote request, newsletter, contact, contest, job application, donation pledge, app submission) we receive the details you enter, such as name, email, phone and message.
  • Automatically collected data — like most websites, our hosting provider and advertising partners may log IP address, browser type, pages visited and referring URLs.
  • Local storage — preferences such as dark mode, upvotes, checklist progress and cookie choice are stored in your own browser and are not sent to us.

How we use information

To respond to you, prepare estimates you request, send newsletters you subscribe to (unsubscribe anytime), run contests, process applications, improve the site and prevent abuse. With your consent, quote requests may be shared with a vetted development partner solely to prepare your estimate. We do not sell personal data.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to us by email through a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit). That service processes the data you submit in order to deliver it.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses cookies in advertising. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info. Where required (e.g. EEA/UK users), consent is requested through a consent message.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you may request access, correction or erasure of your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to us through the contact form (topic: Privacy request).

Retention & security

We keep form data only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, and use reasonable safeguards. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Contest participants under 18 need parental consent.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
