Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to build an app in India?

Simple apps typically start around ₹2–4 lakh, mid-complexity apps with accounts, payments and an admin panel often land between ₹6–15 lakh, and complex marketplaces or AI products can exceed ₹20 lakh. Use our calculator for a starting range, then request an exact quote.

How long does it take?

Most MVPs take 8–16 weeks from final scope to store launch. Complex products take longer and are best released in phases.

Is my idea safe with you?

Yes. Your submission is used only to prepare your estimate and match you with a developer. We're happy to sign an NDA before detailed discussions.

Do you only work with Indian clients?

No. We work with founders in India and abroad who want to build with Indian development talent.

What happens after I submit?

We review your requirements, may ask a few clarifying questions, and send a cost and timeline estimate — usually within one business day.
