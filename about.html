About AppBharat

India has one of the world's largest smartphone audiences, and the apps on those phones now handle money, identity, health records, travel and education. Choosing the right app — and avoiding the wrong one — matters more than ever.

AppBharat is an independent guide that helps people in India find trustworthy apps, understand official government services, switch to Made-in-India alternatives where it makes sense, and stay safe from app-based fraud. We also help founders and businesses turn ideas into apps through free tools and a vetted developer network.

What we do

  • Curate a directory of popular Indian and government apps, linking only to official sources
  • Publish plain-English guides on safety, government apps and building apps
  • Offer free tools: cost calculator, app safety check, comparisons and more
  • Run the AppBharat Build Challenge for students and indie developers
  • Connect founders with developers through free quote requests

Our editorial standards

Standard listings are free and based on usefulness, safety and transparency of the developer. Sponsored placements are always labelled. We don't list real-money gaming, betting or unregistered lending apps. If you spot an error, tell us and we'll fix it.

How we're funded

Display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labelled sponsorships, referral fees from app development projects, and reader support. Advertisers never influence our editorial content.

Independence

AppBharat is a private, independent website. It is not a government website and is not affiliated with any ministry, NPCI, UIDAI, app store or app developer. See our disclaimer & trademark notice.
