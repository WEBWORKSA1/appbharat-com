Naming tips that work in India

  • Short (2–3 syllables), easy to say in Hindi and English.
  • Check the Play Store, App Store and the trademark registry (ipindia.gov.in) before you commit.
  • Grab a matching .com or .in domain and social handles early.
  • Avoid names implying government endorsement (e.g. "Sarkari", national emblems).
